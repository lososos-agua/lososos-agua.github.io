Personal camera integration

Home Lab SDM

View your authorized Google Nest cameras in Home Lab on iPhone, with a companion running on your own Mac.

What it does

Home Lab SDM connects to the Google Smart Device Management API after the account owner grants access. It lists supported cameras and starts a live view only when you choose a camera and press Play. One camera plays at a time, with audio muted initially and a limited session duration.

The Mac companion handles Google authorization and camera connection requests. The iPhone receives the live video and, if you unmute it, camera audio through WebRTC. The current setup requires the Mac to remain awake and reachable from the iPhone on the same local network.

Permissions and control

Google's authorization screen lets you select the home, cameras and available permissions. The camera connection uses live-stream access and associated basic home information, which may include the home name, room names and postal code. This integration does not request event images, recordings or thermostat control.

Connecting, restoring a saved connection and refreshing the camera list do not start video. Home Lab SDM does not record video, take snapshots, send microphone audio or change camera settings.

Personal use

This is an independent, noncommercial project operated by Los Osos for personal use. It is not open for public account registration. These information pages do not sign you in to Google or access your cameras.

Home Lab also has a separate Google Home connection for other home devices. This page and the linked policies describe the SDM camera connection only.

Privacy and support

Google refresh authorization is stored in the Mac's login Keychain so the companion can restore the connection. Google credentials are not placed in the iPhone app or this public website. Read the Home Lab SDM privacy policy for data handling, retention and deletion details.

Use of this personal integration is described in the Home Lab SDM terms of use. For support or privacy requests, contact los.osos.agua@gmail.com.
