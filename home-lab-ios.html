Home Lab for iOS

A household dashboard for viewing authorized Google Home devices and requesting supported light and thermostat controls on iPhone and iPad.

What it does

Home Lab uses the official Google Home iOS SDK to read the homes, rooms and devices your Google authorization makes available. It displays reported device states, connection information and supported capabilities.

You can request power or brightness changes for supported lights. Thermostat temperature and mode changes require the visible Apply action. Available capabilities, device state and supported ranges are checked before a control request is sent.

Connecting, restoring a saved connection, selecting a home and refreshing information do not send device-control commands. This native connection does not create automations or commission new devices.

Your Google authorization

The Google Home SDK manages the Google authorization flow and access to your permitted home devices. You choose the access offered by Google's consent screen and can later review or revoke it. Device queries and control requests use Google's SDK and Google Home infrastructure, including a home hub where applicable.

Personal household use

Home Lab is an independent, noncommercial project operated by Los Osos for the owner and a small number of authorized family members. It does not offer public account registration. These public information pages do not sign you in to Google or access your home.

Scope of these pages

This page and its linked policies describe the native Google Home connection only. Home Lab's separately authorized Nest SDM camera, Ring camera, Calendar and voice connections have separate data flows and are outside this policy's scope.

Privacy and support

Read the Home Lab iOS privacy policy for information about Google Home data, on-device storage and removing access. The terms of use describe this personal integration.
