Privacy policy

Home Lab's native Google Home connection for iPhone and iPad

Last updated October 23, 2026.

1. Scope and operator

This policy covers the native Google Home connection in Home Lab, operated by Los Osos for personal use by the owner and authorized family members. There is no public account registration.

This policy does not cover Home Lab's separately authorized Nest SDM camera, Ring camera, Calendar or voice connections. In particular, the data handling described here is limited to Google Home device access and is not a description of all audio or camera features elsewhere in Home Lab.

2. Information accessed

After you grant Google access, this connection can process the information that the Google Home SDK makes available under your authorization:

  • Home and room names, identifiers and room membership.
  • Device names, identifiers, device types, reported connectivity and supported capabilities.
  • Reported light states and attributes, such as power and brightness, and available thermostat measurements, modes and temperature settings.
  • Your requested device controls and the reported results needed to show their status.
  • Authorization information managed by the Google Home SDK to maintain your chosen account's access.

Google presents and enforces the available home and device permissions for the account you select. This native connection does not obtain Nest SDM camera streams, Ring streams or Calendar events through that authorization.

3. Purpose and processing

The information is used to restore your existing authorized connection, display the available homes and devices, keep reported states up to date, and carry out device controls that you request. Supported light power and brightness controls are user initiated. Thermostat changes require the visible Apply action. The app checks the target and available capability, state and range information before sending a control request.

Connecting, restoring, changing the selected home and refreshing information do not send device-control commands. This native connection does not create automations or commission devices.

Queries and control requests are handled through the Google Home SDK and Google Home infrastructure, which may include a hub on your home network. The native connection's Google authorization credentials are not sent to the separate Mac camera companion or this public website.

4. On-device storage and retention

Home Lab keeps its current home and device inventory in app memory. Disconnecting this connection clears Home Lab's in-memory connection and inventory. The Google Home SDK and iOS may also persist authorization information and cache device metadata on the device as part of their operation. A local disconnect does not promise erasure of all SDK-managed or operating-system storage.

Development diagnostics may contain aggregate counts, capability indicators and fixed status categories. They do not include home or device names and identifiers, passwords, authorization tokens, raw SDK error descriptions or device media. Diagnostic files in app storage or owner-saved copies may remain until they are deleted. Google and operating-system services manage their own records under their policies and settings.

5. Data use and sharing

Home Lab does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, profiling or AI model training. This native connection does not upload the Google Home inventory to analytics or AI services, and does not maintain a separate hosted account database. Google receives the requests needed to provide the authorized home-device features.

Home Lab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Disconnecting, revoking access and deletion

Use Home Lab's Disconnect control to end the local native Google Home session. This does not delete devices from Google Home and does not itself revoke the app's Google authorization across accounts or devices.

To revoke Google authorization, open Google Account connections and remove Home Lab's connection for the relevant account. Revoking that app authorization applies across app instances using the account and can interrupt Home Lab on other household devices connected through it.

For removal of local app data, SDK-managed connection information or saved diagnostic reports, contact the operator for assistance. Uninstalling the app alone may leave operating-system Keychain state. A local-data removal request is separate from deleting the home or devices in Google Home.

7. This website and support

These are static information pages hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no Google sign-in form, public account database, advertising or owner-added analytics. The website does not receive the native app's home inventory or Google authorization credentials.

If you contact support, the operator receives your email address and message and uses them to respond. Support correspondence may be retained while handling your request; contact the operator if you would like it removed. Please do not send passwords or authorization tokens.

8. Contact and changes

For questions or deletion assistance, email los.osos.agua@gmail.com. Changes to this connection's data handling will be reflected on this page, with additional authorization requested when required for new access.
