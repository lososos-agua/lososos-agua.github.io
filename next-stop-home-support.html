Support for the iPhone and iPad puzzle game.

Updated October 4, 2026. The game is in internal TestFlight testing, not public release.

Contact

Email mystudydenllc@gmail.com, or send feedback from your TestFlight invitation. Include the app version and build number, device model, iOS version, stage number, and the last few actions. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, account credentials or unrelated personal information.

Find all 60 stages

Home line contains ten chapters with six stages each. Use the chapter arrows or the chapter title menu to move between them. Every chapter is available from the start. The first release is planned for these 60 stages only.

Try an optional test ad

In an ad-test build, choose a stage you have not completed or already unlocked a full route for, then open the lightbulb hint panel. Open Full route options, select the appropriate eligibility choice, and eligible players can select Watch test ad for full route. The first stage and completed or previously unlocked routes do not require ads.

Google's demo ads need an internet connection and generate no revenue for the game. Complete the ad's reward requirement and close it to return to the full route. Cancelling or a loading error leaves the route locked and does not change your ride. Up to three routes can be unlocked by rewarded ads in a rolling 24 hours. Basic hints, undo and retry remain free.

If no ad button appears, check that you have the ad-test build, that the stage is not already completed or unlocked, and that optional ads have not been declined. Do not erase your progress just to test an ad; choose another unfinished stage. A full-route solution starts from the beginning of the stage, not from your current board position.

Saved rides

Continue restores your saved ride. Undo reverses a turn and Retry starts that stage again. Settings > Erase local progress removes saved progress and guide unlocks on this device; that action cannot be undone. There is no game-account sign-in or cross-device progress service.

Privacy

Read the game's privacy policy for local saves, optional test ads and provider data processing.
