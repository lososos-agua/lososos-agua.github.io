Privacy policy

Home Lab SDM

Effective October 16, 2026.

1. Scope and operator

This policy covers the personal Google Smart Device Management (SDM) camera connection in Home Lab, operated by Los Osos. It consists of an iPhone app and a companion on the owner's Mac. It does not cover Home Lab's separate Google Home connection for other devices, or the unrelated Personal Assistant integration elsewhere on this website. Home Lab SDM is not open for public account registration.

2. Information accessed

After the account owner grants Google access, the camera connection can process:

  • Authorized device identifiers, device categories, connection status and supported camera capabilities.
  • Associated basic home information permitted by Google, which may include home and room names and a postal code. The current camera interface uses generic camera labels and does not display a postal code.
  • Live video and camera audio for a camera the user explicitly chooses to play, plus temporary connection information needed for that session.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to maintain the authorized Google connection.

The SDM integration does not request event photos, recorded video history or thermostat control. It does not capture the iPhone or Mac microphone or camera, transmit microphone audio, record the received video, take snapshots or change camera settings.

3. Purpose and processing

Information is used to authenticate the authorized account, list supported cameras, restore that connection and provide the requested live view. Camera playback starts only after an explicit user action. A connection or inventory refresh does not start a stream.

The Mac communicates with Google's authorization and SDM services. The iPhone communicates with the Mac through an authenticated, encrypted connection and receives live media through WebRTC. Google and its streaming infrastructure process the requests and media needed to provide their service. Home Lab SDM does not operate a separate hosted account database or upload the stream to an analytics or AI service.

4. Local storage and retention

The Mac stores the Google refresh token in its login Keychain, in a Home Lab entry scoped to the camera configuration. The access token, authorized device identifiers and active stream information are kept in process memory. Stopping the companion clears that memory, but retains the saved refresh authorization for the next start.

The Google OAuth client secret is kept in an owner-only configuration file on the Mac. The iPhone stores its separate Mac pairing credential and certificate pin in the app's own Keychain entry. Google access tokens, refresh tokens and client secrets are not included in the iPhone app or the public website.

Received video and audio are temporarily buffered for playback and are not intentionally saved by the integration. Diagnostic reports are limited to device categories, capability flags, counts and fixed status or playback measurements; they exclude credentials, home and device identifiers, names, stream connection contents and media. Reports that the owner saves may remain until the owner deletes them. Google and operating-system services maintain their own records under their policies and settings.

5. Data use and sharing

Home Lab SDM does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or profiling, or use it to train generalized AI models. It uses and transfers Google information only to provide the authorized camera features and associated security or legal requirements. The camera integration does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Home Lab SDM's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revocation and deletion

You can change camera permissions or disconnect Home Lab SDM through Google's Partner Connections Manager. You can also review or remove the connection in Google Account connections. Removing access prevents future access through that authorization; it does not delete local files automatically.

On the Mac companion's connection page, use the control to delete the saved Google connection. This removes the matching Home Lab refresh-token entry from the Mac Keychain and clears the current connection and inventory. If the Keychain is locked or access is denied, unlock it and retry until deletion is confirmed. This local deletion does not itself revoke Google's permission.

To remove the complete local setup, stop the companion and remove its configuration and any saved diagnostic reports or backups. The owner can remove the iPhone's Home Lab SDM pairing entry as part of local data removal; uninstalling an app alone may leave Keychain data. Contact the operator for help completing local deletion. No camera recordings need to be deleted from Home Lab SDM because the integration does not create them.

7. This website and support

These are static information pages hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no Google login form, app database, advertising or owner-added analytics. GitHub may process visitor information to serve and secure the website as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email support, the operator receives your email address and message. Please do not send passwords, tokens or private camera footage. Support correspondence is retained as needed to respond and may be deleted on request, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Contact and changes

For questions or deletion assistance, email los.osos.agua@gmail.com. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy and will require any additional consent applicable to the new features.
