Terms of use

Home Lab's native Google Home connection for iPhone and iPad

Last updated October 23, 2026.

1. Personal household project

Home Lab is an independent, noncommercial project operated by Los Osos for the owner and a small number of authorized family members. It is not open for public account registration and does not offer a paid service. These terms cover its native Google Home connection only, rather than its separate camera, Calendar or voice connections.

2. Authorized use

Use this connection only with accounts, homes and devices you own or are permitted to access. You choose the permissions offered in Google's authorization flow and remain responsible for the device controls you request. Use of Google's services remains subject to the applicable Google terms.

3. Device information and controls

The connection uses the official Google Home iOS SDK to read authorized home, room and device information and reported device states. Supported light power and brightness changes are user initiated. Thermostat changes require the visible Apply action. Available device capabilities, current state and supported ranges are checked before control requests.

Connecting, restoring an existing connection, selecting a home and refreshing information do not send device-control commands. This native connection does not create automations or commission new devices. Available features depend on the permissions granted and the information and capabilities exposed by the device and Google Home.

4. Connection and availability

The connection depends on the Google Home SDK, Google services, device availability, network connectivity and any applicable home hub. Displayed states are reports received from that system. A submitted request alone does not prove that a physical device changed state.

This personal project has no service-level commitment. Features and availability may change as device support, software and Google service requirements change.

5. Privacy and ending use

The Home Lab iOS privacy policy describes the information processed, local storage, Google authorization and deletion assistance.

You may stop using this connection at any time. Local disconnection ends the local session; revoking Home Lab through Google Account connections applies to the app's authorization across app instances using that account. Follow the privacy policy or contact the operator for help removing local data.

6. Contact and changes

For support or questions about these terms, email los.osos.agua@gmail.com. Updates to these terms will be published on this page with an updated date.

Home Lab is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
