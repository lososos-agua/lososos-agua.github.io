Privacy policy

Los Osos Personal Assistant

Last updated September 21, 2026.

1. Scope and operator

This policy describes the Google-connected personal automation operated by Los Osos, referred to here as the assistant. It also distinguishes that integration from this static information website. The assistant is intended for the owner's personal Google account and is not open for public registration.

2. Google information accessed

Depending on the permissions granted and the task requested, the integration can access:

  • Basic account identity, including the authorized email address.
  • Gmail message content, metadata, attachments, labels, and mailbox settings covered by the granted permissions.
  • Calendar events and related scheduling information.
  • Contacts, other contacts, and directory information where available to the account.
  • Drive file metadata and contents, Google Docs documents, and Google Sheets spreadsheets.

Some granted permissions allow changes as well as reading. The assistant uses them in response to the owner's requests or enabled automations; Gmail sending and replies require confirmation.

3. Purposes

Google information is used for requested email search and summaries, scheduling, contact lookup, document and spreadsheet work, and related personal reports or automations. The static website does not perform these tasks or independently connect to a visitor's Google account.

4. Processing and transfers

The assistant runs through local tools, including the gog client and configured automation or AI applications. The current automation uses OpenAI cloud models and can use local models through Ollama. Relevant Google content may be included in requests to the AI service selected for a task. Task results may also be sent to an owner-selected messaging service, such as Telegram, when delivery is part of the requested workflow.

External providers can process and retain the data they receive under their applicable service terms and the owner's account settings. Local execution does not mean all content stays on the local computer. This policy does not promise zero retention by external services. The owner must use provider settings and services that are compatible with Google's Limited Use requirements before sending Google-derived content.

5. Storage and retention

The local gog client's OAuth tokens are stored using macOS Keychain. Tokens and client secrets are not part of this website or its public repository.

Task histories, cached content, generated documents, reports, logs, and backups can contain Google-derived information. Local records may remain until the owner removes them or a configured cleanup process deletes them. There is no single automatic deletion period across these tools. Receiving services and backups have separate retention controls.

6. Use restrictions

Use is limited to the owner's requested personal tasks. Google user data must not be sold, used for advertising, or used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI models. These restrictions apply when selecting external AI services and their data-control settings, as well as to local processing.

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Publishing this policy does not represent Google verification or certify the configuration of an external AI account.

7. Access revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration's access in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future access through that authorization but does not itself erase previously created files, local histories, backups, or records retained by external providers. Those copies require separate deletion through the applicable tools or services.

8. This website

The website consists of static information pages. It includes no Google sign-in form, application database, advertising scripts, or owner-added analytics. When deployed on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Contact and changes

Questions about the integration or this policy can be directed to los.osos.agua@gmail.com. Material changes to the integration's data handling require an updated policy and any additional consent required by Google.
