Privacy policy

Next Stop: Home for iPhone and iPad

Effective October 4, 2026. Current scope: internal TestFlight beta with optional Google demo ads.

Game data on your device

The game saves your current ride, undo history, completed stages, full-route unlocks, reward timestamps, sound and haptic settings, and optional-ad eligibility choice on your device. The game does not require a game account or access to your contacts, precise location, microphone or camera. We do not operate a game-progress server or include a separate analytics SDK.

Optional test advertising

The internal ad-test build uses Google's demonstration ad units. These ads generate no advertising revenue for this game, but still use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and an internet connection. Ad requests are made when a player who has chosen the 16-or-older option selects the test-ad button. Declining ads or selecting the under-16 option does not request an ad.

Google's SDK may process an IP address, app- or device-related identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic or performance information. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's privacy policy. Requesting non-personalized ads does not mean no data is processed. The game does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Demo mode does not exercise this publisher's live regional consent messages. It is not a live-ad deployment or proof of consent compliance. This policy and the release configuration will be reviewed before any live advertising is enabled.

Your choices

All 60 stages, basic hints, retry and undo remain free without advertising. An earned ad reward unlocks the selected stage's complete route on this device. Declining, cancelling or an unavailable ad does not unlock an unfinished route. The first stage, completed stages and previously unlocked routes remain available without another ad.

Optional ads can be turned off in Settings. The game stores only an eligibility choice, not a date of birth. Players who choose the under-16 path are not offered ads. A local age choice is not a substitute for any additional child-privacy requirements that may apply to a future release.

Retention and deletion

Use Settings > Erase local progress to remove the current ride, completed-stage records and unlocked routes. This action does not erase preference settings, operating-system backups, or information already processed by Apple or Google. Providers have their own retention and deletion controls. We do not promise a single retention period or zero collection by those providers.

TestFlight and support

Apple operates TestFlight under its own terms and privacy policy. Feedback you choose to submit through TestFlight may be shared with the developer. When you email support, we receive the email address and information you include and use them to address your request. Please do not send passwords, payment information or unrelated personal data.

This website

These are static information pages with no site-added advertising scripts or analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the website; GitHub's processing is described in its privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent us, contact Next Stop: Home support at mystudydenllc@gmail.com. We will update this page when the app's data handling changes. This internal-beta notice is not an announcement of public App Store availability.
