Los Osos Personal Assistant

A Google-connected assistant for the owner's everyday tasks, operated through local automation tools.

What the assistant does

The assistant uses the local gog client to access Google services after the account owner authorizes them. It supports owner-directed requests and configured automations.

Email
Find and summarize messages, organize labels, and prepare drafts. Sending or replying requires the owner's confirmation.
Calendars and contacts
Read and manage events and contact information for requested scheduling and personal organization tasks.
Drive, Docs, and Sheets
Find files and read, create, or update documents and spreadsheets for requested tasks and reports.

Who can use it

This integration is for the owner's personal use. It is not a public email service, and this website does not offer account registration or request Google access from visitors.

Google access and privacy

Google authorization happens separately from this website. Authorized content may be processed by local tools and configured AI services to complete tasks. The website does not contain Google credentials or expose the owner's mailbox, calendar, contacts, or files.

Read the Personal Assistant privacy policy for the data categories, processing, storage, and access controls.
